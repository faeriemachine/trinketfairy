Description

Zenith is a medium-sized serval plush with short tan-coloured and black spotted fur all over his body. His safety eyes have brown irises, and are lidded with brown paint on top. His nose is made of pleather, but has worn away from age and use. He has large ears with long tufts of fur coming out of them. He has a white muzzle and belly, and white around his eyes. His limbs are long and dangly with beans in each paw, and his tail is mid-length. He also has beans in his bottom.

He wears a rainbow-coloured collar with kandi bracelets on his arms, and he's sometimes seen wearing a navy-blue, lightning bolt patterned pet vest, with a green textured Tangle toy and a carabiner attached to it.
