Nutmeg is a medium-large sized, pastel-orange cat, made out of fuzzy fabric that's covered in a pattern of pumpkins. Her safety eyes have brown irises, and she has darker orange fleece-like fabric on the bottom of her paws and inside her ears. She has soft threaded whiskers on her muzzle, and her nose is pink and flocked (but degraded with age and use).

She wears a kandi necklace around her neck.
